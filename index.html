Privacy Policy

Overview

VoltSpend is designed to work entirely on your device. There are no user accounts, no sign-up, and no VoltSpend server that your data is sent to. Everything you enter or scan — appliances, electricity rate settings, and timer history — is stored locally on your device using standard on-device storage, and stays there unless you delete the app or your device's own backup system (e.g. iCloud or Google account backup) includes app data as part of a full device backup.

Camera and photos

VoltSpend requests camera access for two scanning features: photographing an appliance's power or energy label, and photographing a page of your electricity bill to read your rates. Photo library access lets you choose an existing photo instead of using the camera directly, for either feature. In both cases, the photo is processed entirely on your device using on-device text recognition (Apple's or Google's on-device OCR, depending on platform) to read whatever's in it. Photos are never uploaded to a server by VoltSpend and are not sent anywhere outside your device by the app itself. A photo is only used at the moment you scan it — VoltSpend doesn't keep a copy of it, or of an electricity bill photo specifically, afterward.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

VoltSpend uses Google AdMob (react-native-google-mobile-ads) to show ads. AdMob is a third-party service operated by Google that is not controlled by VoltSpend. VoltSpend does not request permission to track you (no App Tracking Transparency prompt) and explicitly requests non-personalised ads from Google, so ads are not based on your advertising identifier or cross-app activity. Even so, to serve any ad at all Google AdMob may collect and process information such as your IP address, approximate location derived from it, general app and device information, and non-personalised interaction data, in accordance with Google's own privacy policy. VoltSpend does not receive or store this data itself — it is collected and processed directly by Google as the advertising provider.

Notifications

If you turn on a cost or time limit for an appliance, VoltSpend schedules a local notification on your device to alert you as you approach or pass that limit. These notifications are generated and delivered entirely on your device — they are not sent from a VoltSpend server (VoltSpend doesn't operate one), and no data about your usage leaves your device to make them happen. You can turn notification permission off at any time in your device's system settings; limits will still show an in-app warning while you're using the app, just without the background alert.

Exporting, importing, and sharing your data

VoltSpend lets you export everything you've stored in the app to a file, and later import that file back in — on this device or another. An export only leaves your device if and when you actively choose a destination in your device's own share sheet (for example, saving it to Files, or sending it to yourself); VoltSpend does not upload it anywhere automatically. Sharing a cost result as an image works the same way — nothing is sent anywhere until you pick where to send it. Importing a file replaces or merges data already on your device, entirely locally; VoltSpend never reads this data for any purpose other than restoring it into the app at your request.

Appliance lookup data

The appliance brand/model lookup feature uses publicly available data from the Australian Government's Energy Rating Product Database (data.gov.au), used under a Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Australia licence. This is product registration data (brand, model, energy consumption figures) — it is not personal information, and using this feature does not send any information about you to that database; the data is bundled into the app itself.

Information we don't collect

VoltSpend does not require or collect your name, email address, or any account credentials, as there is no account system. VoltSpend does not track you across other apps or websites, and does not sell personal information, because it does not operate a service that collects personal information from you in the first place.

Children's privacy

VoltSpend is a general household utility tool and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app has no accounts and stores data locally, no personal information is knowingly collected from anyone, including children, other than what a device's advertising SDK (Google AdMob, above) may process on Google's side.

Your rights and choices

Since all VoltSpend data lives on your device, you have direct control over it at all times: delete an appliance from within the app, use Settings to export a backup, reset the app to remove everything and start fresh, or uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data at once. If you have concerns about how Google AdMob processes data on Google's side, Google's privacy policy explains your choices, including ad personalisation controls in your device settings. If you believe an Australian Privacy Principle has been breached and the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) applies to this app, you may lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

Changes to this policy

If VoltSpend's data practices change (for example, if a future feature introduces an optional account or cloud sync), this policy will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
